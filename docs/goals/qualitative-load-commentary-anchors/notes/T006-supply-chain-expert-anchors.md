# T006 Supply Chain And Infrastructure-Expert Commentary Anchors

## Receipt Summary

Gathered high-signal supply-chain and infrastructure-expert anchors for data-center construction-rate bottlenecks.

Strongest evidence is from primary sources:

- Nvidia, TSMC, SK hynix, and Micron for AI compute, HBM, and advanced packaging supply.
- Vertiv, Eaton, Schneider Electric, Siemens Energy, GE Vernova, Prysmian, and Quanta for power, cooling, grid equipment, and EPC backlog.
- CBRE, JLL, Uptime Institute, and IEA for expert cross-market constraints.

Most commentary indicates demand acceleration but physical bottlenecks in power access, transformers/switchgear, turbines, cooling, permitting, and grid connections. None should be treated as direct energized-MW proof.

## Notes For Judge

- Semiconductor sell-in/backlog and packaging expansion are upstream evidence; they do not prove data-center buildings are complete or energized.
- CBRE/JLL show record demand and preleasing while also reporting power, permitting, zoning, and supply-chain delays.
- GE Vernova, Siemens Energy, Eaton, and Prysmian backlog growth is both a demand signal and a constrained-delivery signal.
- IEA says data-center electricity use surged in 2025 while many onsite gas-power projects remain early-stage and technically/financially constrained.
- SemiAnalysis-style commentary was intentionally deprioritized because stable public/primary/expert institutional sources were enough for the first slice.
